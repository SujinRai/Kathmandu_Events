About Us

Discover the story behind Kathmandu Events.

Who We Are

Kathmandu Events is a platform designed to connect people with events happening throughout Kathmandu. We aim to make discovering local activities easier and more accessible.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring the community together by providing information about ongoing and upcoming events, including concerts, festivals, workshops, sports and cultural programs.

Our Vision

We envision a Kathmandu where everyone can easily discover exciting opportunities, participate in community activities and experience the city's culture and entertainment.
